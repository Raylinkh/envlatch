EnvLatch signed and notarized release

This disk image and EnvLatch.app are signed with a Developer ID Application
certificate, notarized by Apple, and stapled for offline Gatekeeper checks.

Double-click "Install EnvLatch.command" to transactionally install:

- EnvLatch.app under ~/Applications
- the envlatch CLI under ~/.local/bin
- the shared agent skill under ~/.agents/skills

The installer never copies or exports saved secret values. EnvLatch keeps them
in macOS Keychain and releases selected values only to a command launched with
envlatch run.

https://github.com/Raylinkh/envlatch
